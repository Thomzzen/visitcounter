About gadgetweekly

gadgetweekly is a small, one-person project: a running list of things worth buying, picked because they're genuinely fun or handy — not because someone paid for the placement.

Every week (roughly) a new Top 10 goes up. What started as gadgets has room to grow into other categories too — check the Categories menu above to see what's live right now.

Nothing here is sponsored unless a post says so directly, and every affiliate link is disclosed on its own page. Got something worth featuring, or a question about a pick? Reach out.
